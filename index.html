GLOWGUIDE FIELD NOTES

How Glow score works

GlowGuide treats each routine as a basket of products and mines recurring co-usage patterns. Apriori builds frequent itemsets level by level; FP-Growth compresses baskets into a prefix tree and mines conditional patterns.

Glow score100 × confidence × (0.65 + 0.35 × min(lift, 3) / 3) × support reliabilitySupport reliability grows as a pattern appears in more transactions. It is a ranking aid, not a clinical score.

Routine context comparison checks the same pair in morning and evening baskets, then labels a pattern by its stronger context. The dataset is synthetic; discovered rules demonstrate the implementation and should not guide medical decisions.
